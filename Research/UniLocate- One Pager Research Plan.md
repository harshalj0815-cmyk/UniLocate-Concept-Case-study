| Research question | Why it is hard or takes so long for student to navigate on new college campuses? |
| What I assume | 1. The campuses are to complex and confusing to understand on first day<br/>2. Lack of signs and physical name tags to the places<br/>3. The physical maps are static and can be access from one particular point |
| Primary method + why | The primary method I'll use is 1:1 user interviews (semi-structured, conversational). I chose this method because I want to understand Why it is hard or takes so long for student to navigate on new college campuses. Interviews let me capture their confusion in the college navigation, their emotions, their thinking, and edge cases. |
| Secondary / triangulation source | Survey to understand the proportion of users who have gone through the same issue and how it affected them so we can define the success clearly. |
| Who I’ll talk to | 18 to 22 year old students who are new to the college and students who often visits different colleges regularly for events |
| “Enough” signal | When the same pattern, behavior, reason, or emotion shows up across 2-3 of the people I talk to. |
