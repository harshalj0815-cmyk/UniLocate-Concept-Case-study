**Warm-up**

1. Tell me a bit about yourself. What are you studying, and which year are you in?

**Context & background**

1.  What does your daily college routine look like?
2. How did you prepare for your first day, if at all?

**Core episode**

1. Walk me through your first week of college, especially how you found your classrooms.
2. Tell me about a time you couldn’t find your class. What happened?
    1. What did you do next?
    2. How long did it take to find it?
3.  What was going through your mind at that moment?
4. Who, if anyone, did you ask for help?
5. Tell me about a time when finding your class took longer than you expected. What happened?
6. What did you use, if anything, to find your way around campus?
    1. Did you use anything like a map, signs, or an app?
7. Tell me about a time when finding a place felt easy.
    1. What made it easy?
8. How has the way you find places changed since your first week?
9. Tell me about a time you saw another student who was lost. What happened?

**Wrap up**

1. Is there anything I didn’t ask that I should have?
2. Do you know anyone else who had a similar experience that I could talk to?
